Website privacy

This notice applies to the Afsanora studio website at afsanora.com. Our games and their websites have their own privacy policies, linked below.

Browsing this website

This static website does not provide user accounts or contact forms. Optional Google Analytics is loaded only after you accept analytics. You can reject it and still use the site.

The hosting provider may process technical information, such as IP addresses and request logs, to deliver the website and maintain security. The website is hosted using GitHub Pages. See GitHub's privacy statement for its handling of visitor information.

Optional website analytics

With your consent, Google Analytics measures visits, device and browser information, approximate location, returning visits and clicks to play or download our games. In the browser game, it also measures game mode, stage number, board size, wins and losses, and active play time. We do not send your name, email, saved puzzles, or URL query strings. Google receives technical connection information and uses a cookie identifier to distinguish browsers. Counts represent consenting browsers, not verified individual people.

Your analytics choice is saved for 180 days across afsanora.com and its subdomains. Use Privacy choices to change it at any time. Rejecting stops future collection and removes accessible Analytics cookies; it does not delete previously collected data. Advertising personalization and Google signals are disabled. See Google privacy information. This consent is separate from mobile-app advertising consent.

Contacting us

If you email us, we receive your email address and the information you choose to include. We use that information to respond to your enquiry and provide support. Please do not include passwords or sensitive personal information. To ask about your correspondence or request its deletion, contact pathora@afsanora.com.

Games and external links

Opening a game, visiting a game website or following an external link takes you to a separate service. Its data practices may differ from this studio website. Read the Pathora Privacy Policy and the Lightora Privacy Policy for details about each game.

Changes

We will update this notice if the studio website's data practices change, including if advertising or analytics are added.
