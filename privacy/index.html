Website privacy

This notice applies to the Afsanora studio website at afsanora.com. Our games and their websites have their own privacy policies, linked below.

Browsing this website

This is a static information website. It does not provide user accounts, collect information through forms, or currently load advertising or analytics scripts. We do not set cookies on this studio website.

The hosting provider may process technical information, such as IP addresses and request logs, to deliver the website and maintain security. The website is hosted using GitHub Pages. See GitHub's privacy statement for its handling of visitor information.

Contacting us

If you email us, we receive your email address and the information you choose to include. We use that information to respond to your enquiry and provide support. Please do not include passwords or sensitive personal information. To ask about your correspondence or request its deletion, contact pathora@afsanora.com.

Games and external links

Opening a game, visiting a game website or following an external link takes you to a separate service. Its data practices may differ from this studio website. Read the Pathora Privacy Policy and the Lightora Privacy Policy for details about each game.

Changes

We will update this notice if the studio website's data practices change, including if advertising or analytics are added.
